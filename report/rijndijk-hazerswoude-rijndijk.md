**Foundation risk indication**

- 224 of 582 addresses are in buildings from before 1970, on clay or peat soils. Buildings of that age on soft soil often stand on wooden piles or shallow foundations, which can deteriorate with falling groundwater (pile rot) or subsidence. Ask for a foundation survey (funderingsonderzoek) for any pre-1970 property here.
- Soil contamination (Bodemloket) is not yet included; see open items.

## Buyer rules and starter loans

- **Starterslening (SVn)** (onbevestigd, waarschijnlijk niet beschikbaar): Two third-party sites (Libero, HomeFinance) say Alphen aan den Rijn does not currently offer an SVn starterslening. One other site says it does. No official gemeente page was found. Note: the €420,000 limit that shows up in searches belongs to Alphen-Chaam, a different municipality. Sources: [Starterslening Alphen aan den Rijn (HomeFinance)](https://www.homefinance.nl/hypotheek/starters/starterslening-alphen-aan-den-rijn/) (unofficial); [Starterslening Alphen aan den Rijn (Libero)](https://www.liberofinancieel.nl/starterslening/alphen-aan-den-rijn) (unofficial)
- **Opkoopbescherming** (niet ingevoerd (volgens nieuwsberichten en een raadsvoorstel uit 2023)): The council did not introduce opkoopbescherming; a 2023 raadsvoorstel called it disproportionate. A zelfbewoningsplicht (owner-occupancy obligation) applies to new-build. The regional Huisvestingsverordening Holland Rijnland 2026 has not yet been checked for buyer rules. Sources: [Raadsvoorstel 2023, Alphen aan den Rijn](https://alphenaandenrijn.bestuurlijkeinformatie.nl/Document/View/22d038a1-20b3-4331-be82-0eda4b1fd897); [Huisvestingsverordening Holland Rijnland 2026](https://alphenaandenrijn.bestuurlijkeinformatie.nl/Document/View/8d16fe58-431b-4f6a-91d8-cf0ac7f41b22); [Grip op de woningmarkt: wel zelfbewoningsplicht, geen opkoopbescherming (Studio Alphen)](https://www.studioalphen.nl/nieuws/grip-op-de-woningmarkt-wel-zelfbewoningsplicht-geen-opkoopbescherming/) (unofficial)
- **NHG-kostengrens**: €470.000 (2026) Sources: [Starterslening 2026 (Starter.nl), quoting NHG](https://starter.nl/starterslening-2026/) (unofficial)
- **SVn Starterslening, landelijke kaders**: At most 20% of the purchase price or valuation, and never above the gemeente's maximum (minimum €2,500). Rate 4.40%, fixed for 15 years, on the date checked. Equity limit €57,684 (single) or €115,368 (fiscal partners). Sources: [Starterslening: extra lenen naast hypotheek (Consumentenbond)](https://www.consumentenbond.nl/hypotheek/starter/starterslening-extra-lenen-naast-je-maximale-hypotheek) (unofficial)

## Notes and open items

- **Autoluw** is approximated by cars per household and road-noise nuisance. Traffic decisions (verkeersbesluiten), speed limits and parking regimes are planned additions.
- **Energy labels** (EP-Online) need an API key and are not included yet.
- **School inspection verdicts**: DUO's published download link does not resolve; to be fetched from the Inspectie directly. Secondary-school exam grades on DUO stop at 2017, so they are not used.
- **Soil contamination** (Bodemloket) and a 100 m Leefbaarometer grid are planned.
- **Crime** is published per buurt, never per address.
- **Asking prices** come from public listing pages found through online search and are not sale prices.
